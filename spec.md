**System Objective:** To develop an autonomous AI assistant that searches, aggregates, and analyzes property listings in Pune, verifying builder claims against official records and managing communications.

**Target User Profile:** An IT professional aiming to purchase a 2.5 or 3 BHK apartment, prioritizing proximity to IT hubs, clear land titles, and RERA registration.

**Key Functional Modules:**

1.  **Data Ingestion & Aggregation:**
    
    *   Autonomous scraping of Pune-based real estate portals (e.g., Magicbricks, NoBroker).
        
    *   Configurable parameters for apartment type (2.5 or 3 BHK), property status (new construction or resale), and budget constraints.
        
    *   Filtering based on proximity to IT parks and key amenities.
        
2.  **Verification & Analysis Engine:**
    
    *   Cross-references property and builder details with official RERA records.
        
    *   Checks for legal disputes or negative complaints online.
        
    *   Analyzes area distinctions and price per square foot against market trends.
        
    *   Designed as a pluggable, modular architecture allowing for new analytics parameters via natural language interaction.
        
3.  **Digital Communication Interface:**
    
    *   Automated querying through WhatsApp and Email.
        
    *   Requests detailed information such as layout plans and pricing schemes autonomously.
        
    *   Ensures user privacy by not exposing personal contact numbers.
        
4.  **Reporting System:**
    
    *   Provides both scheduled daily summaries and on-demand comprehensive reports.
        
    *   Presents a ranked list of verified and suitable properties, complete with analysis details.
